SVG Shapes
House & Garden Scene

Become familiar with creating and formatting SVG primitives. Use all the different SVG shape types, understand the coordinate system, and apply grouping with <g> elements.

STEP 1Create a Picture with SVG Shapes

Build a house and garden scene using every SVG primitive type: rect, circle, polygon, line, polyline, ellipse, text, and path. The scene below uses them all — no annotations, just the drawing.

LIVE SVG OUTPUT — House & Garden Scene
STEP 2Understand the SVG Coordinate System

SVG uses a top-left origin (0, 0). The x-axis increases rightward and the y-axis increases downward. All shape positions are relative to this origin.

STEP 3Customise Your Picture

Move shapes by changing x, y, cx, cy values. Change fill and stroke colours. Add stroke-width, stroke-dasharray, and opacity.

STEP 4Group Elements with <g>

Both windows share stroke and fill because they are wrapped in a <g> group. Use transform="translate(x, y)" on the group to move all children at once.

<!-- both windows inside a <g> group -->
<g>
  <rect x="286" y="248" width="82" height="66"/>
  <rect x="532" y="248" width="82" height="66"/>
</g>
STEP 5View the DOM in Developer Tools

Open DevTools (F12) → Elements tab. Each SVG element appears as a node in the DOM tree, matching exactly the shapes visible in the canvas.
